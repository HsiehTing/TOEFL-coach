# Take an Interview

Use rubric ID `ets-speaking-blueprint-2026-diagnostic`.

Use either the learner-provided transcript or the path-free local-ASR transcript and four-question Interview structure. Ask only about a missing or ambiguous prompt/answer pair and preserve explicitly labelled pairs. Fewer than 4 completely mapped questions is diagnostic only, not a formal session.

## Required evidence

- Direct answer and relevance
- Idea elaboration through explanation, reason, example, or detail
- Coherence and organization
- Grammar and vocabulary
- Fluency, pausing, and repair — unavailable unless the applicable acoustic evidence is usable
- Pronunciation, stress, intonation, prosody, and intelligibility — unavailable unless the applicable acoustic evidence is usable

Treat 4 questions as one formal session. Do not use word-for-word matching as the primary content measure.

Assign a bounded re-recording of one or two answers targeting the top priorities.
