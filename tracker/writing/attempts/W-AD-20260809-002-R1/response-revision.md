Whether a brand's visual designs and advertising message should be consistent or regularly update its appearance and messaging is an issue that worth discuss about. I believe that regularly updating a company's appearance and message is a better marketing strategy.

As Paul suggests, adapting to changing market trends is crucial for business growth. I also believe that following trends would help a brand become more relevant to customers. For example, during FIFA World Cup, companies like Coca-Cola or McDonald's would release brand-new packages of beverages or meals that match the theme of the sport event. And during the World Cup, the popularity and impression of the two brands is always in high end. The popularity can further leads to sales, because when the customers gets hungry or thirsty while watching the game, the first brand comes to the their mind is the brand that keep up the trends of the World Cup.

Some argue that a company should be consistent with their aesthetic and messaging so that customers can build a stronger recognition and trust. However, if the image stays unchanged, younger customers would find the brand old-fashioned or outdated. When younger customers are looking for something fashionable, it is unlikely for them to consider a brand whose image remained unchanged. So, I believe that changing a company's appearance and message regularly is crucial.
