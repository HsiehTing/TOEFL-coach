Whether a brand's visual designs and advertising message should be consistent or regularly updates its appearance and messaging is an issue that worth discuss about. I believe regularly updates a company's appearance and message is a better marketing strategy.

As Paul suggests, adapting to changing market trends is crucial for business growth. And I also believe that by following the trends would help a brand getting more relevant to customers. For example, during every FIFA World Cup, companies like Coca-Cola or McDonald's would release brand new package of beverages or meal that match the theme of the sport event. And during the World Cup the popularity and impression of the two brands is always in high end and further transform in to sales. According to the statistics, in the year of 2026, 40% of the revenue was gained during the Wold Cup.

Some argues that a company should be consistent with their aesthetic and messaging so that customers can build a stronger recognition and trust. And the method leads repeat customers and recommendations. However, if the image stays unchanged, younger customers would find the brand old-fashioned or outdated. When younger customers are looking for something fashionable, it is unlikely of them to consider the brand that the image remain unchanged since they were child. So, I believe that changing a company's appearance and message regularly is crucial.
