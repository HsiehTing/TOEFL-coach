Which measures governments should take to reduce carbon dioxide emissions from vehicles is an important question. I believe that expanding public transportation is the more effective solution.

As Claire suggests, if governments make public transportation more accessible, cheaper, and easier to use, people will drive less, further reducing air pollution. Although electric cars are useful in rural areas, cities have denser populations, so an expanded bus or rail system can replace many individual car trips at once. Governments should therefore prioritize public transportation in urban areas while continuing to support practical electric-car options for rural residents.
