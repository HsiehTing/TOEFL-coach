You are a university student and a member of the student council at your university. Recently, the university administration announced that they are planning to add new facilities to the campus and want suggestions from students. You have an idea about a facility that would benefit students greatly. You need to recommend this idea to the head of the student council, Ms. Patterson.

Write an email to Ms. Patterson. In your email, do the following:
- Explain the importance of adding new facilities to the campus.
- Describe the specific facility you are proposing and its benefits.
- Request her support in presenting this suggestion to the university administration.
