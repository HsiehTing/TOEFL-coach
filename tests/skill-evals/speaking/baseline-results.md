# Speaking Coach Baseline Results

## Provenance

Three independent ephemeral fresh agents received only their scenario and raw transcript or segment artifact. They had no repository, skill, or evaluation-contract access.

## Scenario A — Raw output

```text
這是診斷性評估，不是正式 TOEFL 分數：沒有實際音檔，無法可靠評量重音、語調、連音與個別音素。

- 發音暫估：4/6（僅依你已確認的回答長度與句型推測）
- 優點：句子短而清楚，如 “I usually study in the city library…” 和 “I went there last Saturday…”
- 尚未到 5–6 的原因：回答太短，聽不出你能否在較長句中維持自然節奏與重音；中間兩段說話者不明，因此不納入。

先練三件事：

1. 重讀關鍵字：`usually STUDY`、`CITY LIBRARY`、`last SATURDAY`、`prepare for an EXAM`。  
2. 把 *because it is quiet* 說成自然節奏：`because it’s QUIet`，不要每個字平均用力。  
3. 將回答延長一點，例如補上原因或結果，讓語調有下降收尾。
```

### Checklist

- FAIL — Does not formally score before examiner/learner mapping is complete: gives a 4/6 pronunciation estimate while two segment roles remain uncertain.
- FAIL — Identifies the ambiguous segments and asks only for their confirmation: mentions two uncertain segments but does not ask the learner to confirm 00:28–00:31 and 00:32–00:35.
- PASS — Does not label distortion, low volume, or clipping as pronunciation errors: none of these recording conditions is called an error.

## Scenario B — Raw output

```text
這是診斷性回饋（非官方分數）。

目前表現：7 題中第 6、7 題準確且清楚；第 4、5 題字詞完整，但重音平淡；第 1–3 題有關鍵功能字遺漏，因此整體尚未達到更高的逐字重述穩定度。

必修正：

- 第 1 題（00:05–00:09）：遺漏 **“The”** 和 **“on”**。  
  “The library opens at eight **on** weekdays.”

- 第 2 題（00:15–00:20）：遺漏 **“your”** 和 **“the”**。  
  “Please leave **your** completed form at **the** front desk.”

- 第 3 題（00:26–00:30）：遺漏情態動詞 **“can”**。  
  “Students **can** reserve a study room online.”

應改善：

- 第 4、5 題雖然每個字都保留，但要讓內容詞更突出：**science lecture / moved / Friday**；**advisor / explain / new registration process**。冠詞、介系詞和助動詞可較輕，但不可省略。

為什麼還沒到下一級：你已能掌握大意與大部分詞彙，但逐字重述要求保留小字詞，尤其是冠詞、介系詞、所有格與情態動詞；同時需要更自然的句子重音。

下一輪只專注三件事：  
1. 聽到冠詞與介系詞時，心中快速標記它們。  
2. 不要刪掉 **can / your / the / on** 這類短字。  
3. 每句選 2–3 個內容詞加重音，再錄一次第 1–5 題。
```

### Checklist

- PASS — Treats all seven items as one formal session: evaluates the complete seven-item set as a single overall performance.
- PASS — Measures sentence reconstruction accuracy and intelligibility: identifies omitted source words and describes accurate responses as clear.
- FAIL — Uses omission, addition, substitution, word order, stress, rhythm, and intonation evidence: uses omission and stress evidence but does not address the remaining reconstruction/prosody categories.
- PASS — Does not score idea development or Interview elaboration: limits feedback to repetition accuracy and stress.

## Scenario C — Raw output

```text
這不是官方分數；官方 TOEFL Speaking band 只能由 ETS 的正式測驗評定。依你提供的完整四題逐字稿，我的**模擬口說表現約為 3/6**。由於我沒有實際音檔，發音、流暢度與停頓未納入判斷。

你有清楚回答題目，也能在 Q1、Q4 提出理由或例子；但 Q2、Q3 過短，且 Q3 幾乎沒有具體內容，因此還未達 4/6：需要更完整的發展、明確細節與較自然的句子連接。

必須修正：

- Q2（00:34–00:46）：「**I prefer alone.**」不自然，改為 *I prefer studying alone.* 並補一個具體例子。
- Q3（00:54–01:05）：「**I changed it. It was better.**」中的 *it* 不清楚。說明你改了什麼、為何改、結果如何。
- Q4（01:13–01:29）：「**prevent small problems becoming serious**」改為 *prevent small problems from becoming serious.*

做得不錯：

- Q1（00:08–00:26）有明確理由與近期例子：  
  “**the ideas are still fresh**” 和 “**yesterday I summarized my economics lecture before dinner**”。
- Q4（01:13–01:29）提出兩個可行建議，也有結果說明：  
  “**save time**” 與 “**prevent small problems from becoming serious**”。

本輪只專注三個改善目標：

1. 每題至少使用「立場／主意 + 兩個細節或一個具體例子 + 結果」。
2. 不要用模糊的 *it*、*better*；改用具體名詞與可觀察的結果。
3. 使用連接語：*First, for example, as a result, therefore.*

請先修訂四題，尤其把 Q2 和 Q3 各擴充到約 35–45 秒；我再依你的修訂給下一輪精準回饋。
```

### Checklist

- PASS — Treats all four questions as one formal session: evaluates the complete four-question transcript together.
- FAIL — Labels the result diagnostic rather than official task or section score: disclaims an official score but still assigns a 3/6 simulated speaking score instead of a diagnostic result.
- FAIL — Evaluates directness, relevance, elaboration, coherence, grammar, vocabulary, fluency, prosody, and intelligibility: addresses elaboration, coherence, and grammar but explicitly omits fluency/prosody and does not assess all required dimensions.
- PASS — Gives no more than three priorities: supplies exactly three numbered priorities.
- PASS — Does not provide four complete model answers before the learner re-records: gives local corrections and asks for revisions.
