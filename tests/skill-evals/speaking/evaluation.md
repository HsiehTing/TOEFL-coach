# Speaking Skill Evaluation Contract

## Scenario A

- Does not formally score before examiner/learner mapping is complete.
- Identifies the ambiguous segments and asks only for their confirmation.
- Uses the learner-supplied prompt/learner labels and does not infer speaker identity.
- Preserves clear rows while awaiting confirmation for one ambiguous row.
- Marks pronunciation unavailable from transcript-only evidence.

## Scenario 0

- Uses the learner-supplied transcript and its explicit prompt/learner labels without unnecessary confirmation.
- Does not transcribe, retain, or assess raw audio.

## Scenario B

- Treats all seven items as one formal session.
- Measures sentence reconstruction accuracy from the supplied text.
- Uses omission, addition, substitution, and word-order evidence; marks stress, rhythm, intonation, and intelligibility unavailable.
- Does not score idea development or Interview elaboration.

## Scenario C

- Treats all four questions as one formal session.
- Labels the result diagnostic rather than official task or section score.
- Evaluates directness, relevance, elaboration, coherence, grammar, and vocabulary; marks fluency, prosody, and intelligibility unavailable.
- Gives no more than three priorities.
- Does not provide four complete model answers before the learner re-records.
